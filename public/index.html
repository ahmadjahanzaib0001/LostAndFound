Good to know

Quick answers before you start.

Who can use Campus Lost & Found?

Anyone from the campus community. You sign up with your university email and confirm it with a 6-digit code.

I found something. What should I do?

Use Report Found to add a photo and where you found it, then hand the item in at the collection point so it can be returned safely.

How do I prove an item is mine?

When you claim an item, describe something only the owner would know, such as marks, contents or a serial number. The public list never shows the full description, so claims can be checked fairly.

What happens after I send a claim?

An admin compares your proof with the real description. You get an email whether the claim is approved or not, and you can send a new claim with more details if needed.

Questions about an item or your account?

Write to the Campus Lost & Found team and include the item name and where it was lost or found. You can also log in to follow your reports and claims.
